// 03 COLLABORATION PROCESS

How I Work with Small Businesses

When planning your campaigns, social media marketing packages should match the channels your audience actually uses. Rather than rigid tiers, social media marketing services packages are structured around four clear stages of collaboration:

1. Discovery & Profile Review

Reviewing your current social media profiles, target customer demographics, and brand positioning to determine which platforms deserve primary focus.

2. Content Planning & Design

Developing a monthly content calendar covering core topics, educational posts, and branded visual assets that match your visual identity.

3. Scheduling & Management

Scheduling approved posts, maintaining consistent publishing intervals, and coordinating seo and social media marketing services to maintain active profiles.

4. Review & Optimization

Reviewing monthly reach, engagement metrics, and audience feedback to adjust post themes and refine messaging over time.
