The 5-Stage Custom Logo Design Process

A disciplined engineering and design methodology ensuring every mark is original, scalable, and versatile.

STEP 01

Discovery & Brief

Defining brand personality, target demographics, competitor differentiators, and core values.

STEP 02

Paper Sketches

Rapid pencil sketching of 30+ visual metaphors to find strong, memorable silhouettes before digitizing.

STEP 03

Vector Construction

Digitizing selected concepts in Adobe Illustrator using geometric balance, clean bezier paths, and monochrome testing.

STEP 04

Typography & Color

Selecting pairing typefaces, manual letter-spacing (kerning), and establishing accessible color palettes.

STEP 05

Stress-Testing & Delivery

Testing favicon clarity at 16px, dark/light contrast, and exporting full master vector packages.
