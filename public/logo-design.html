// 03 DESIGN WORKFLOW

The Logo Design Process

Creating an enduring mark requires a methodical design process. Here is how we develop your company logo together from initial idea to final assets:

1. Discovery & Positioning

Discussing your business mission, target customer base, and the qualities your logo needs to communicate.

2. Concept Exploration

Developing initial black-and-white vector sketches to test silhouette, geometry, and readability at small scales.

3. Color & Typography Selection

Pairing approved marks with purposeful color palettes and complementary brand fonts.

4. Delivery of Vector Assets

Exporting your finalized logo in scalable vector formats (SVG) and transparent PNGs for all digital uses.
