// 03 WORKFLOW & COLLABORATION

How We Collaborate on Design Projects

As an independent graphic design site and portfolio, this graphic artist website represents a direct, collaborative working style without agency overhead. Each design project follows four straightforward steps:

1. Creative Brief & References

Discussing your design requirements, audience preferences, preferred color palettes, and any reference styles you admire.

2. Concept Exploration

Preparing initial layout drafts and typographic concepts to establish the right visual direction before final production.

3. Revisions & Refinement

Refining the chosen draft based on your notes, tuning contrast, typography hierarchy, and spacing.

4. Final Delivery & Asset Export

Delivering web-optimized files in standard formats, ready for publishing across your social media channels and website.
