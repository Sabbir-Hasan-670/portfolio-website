The Creative Design Workflow

How we take your visual ideas from creative brief to final vector delivery.

PHASE 01

Creative Brief

Clarifying visual dimensions, target audience preferences, color rules, and messaging requirements.

PHASE 02

Concept Drafting

Exploring moodboards and layout wireframes to test compositional balance and typography hierarchy.

PHASE 03

Vector Polishing

Executing final vector artwork with optical alignment, color harmony, and platform-compliant ratios.

PHASE 04

Review & Refine

Dedicated client review rounds to adjust copy placement, contrast, or color accents.

PHASE 05

Asset Handoff

Delivery of organized file packages (AI, SVG, transparent PNG, WebP) structured for immediate publishing.
