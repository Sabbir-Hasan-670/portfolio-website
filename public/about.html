About Sabbir Hasan

Computer Science graduate bridging the gap between secure network engineering and full-stack software development.

My Journey & Mission

I graduated with a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering from Lovely Professional University, India, and am based in Faridpur, Bangladesh. With formal training in CCNA 200-301, I specialize in architecting reliable network infrastructures, implementing security policies, and building scalable full-stack applications.

My hands-on network engineering experience includes designing multi-switch topologies, VLAN segmentation, inter-VLAN routing, Access Control Lists (ACLs), Network Address Translation (NAT/PAT), and OSPF routing using Cisco Packet Tracer and GNS3.

Beyond networking, I build production-grade web systems using Node.js, Express, and MySQL, alongside applied cybersecurity lab investigations using Wireshark, Nmap, and Metasploit. Fluent in English, Bangla, and Hindi, I bring strong analytical problem-solving and collaboration to every project.
